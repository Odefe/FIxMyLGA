Community standards

Community Guidelines

These principles describe the intended public community space when FixMyLGA is operating.

Interactive portfolio edition · Updated 22 August 2026

Be accurate and constructive

Share current evidence, describe what you can observe, and correct mistakes promptly.

Protect people’s privacy

Do not publish private contact details, faces where consent is absent, sensitive documents or precise household information.

Keep the focus on physical issues

Use the platform for roads, flooding, waste, sanitation and public infrastructure—not harassment, party campaigning or personal disputes.

No manipulation or abuse

Spam, duplicate accounts, manufactured evidence, threats and attempts to game verification are not acceptable.

Portfolio status

Visitors can try the local reporting walkthrough. Live posting, accounts, verification and moderation queues remain paused.
