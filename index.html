How it works

From a local problem to a public record of progress.

01

Report

Share a local physical problem with current photos, video and location evidence.

02

Verify

Nearby community members help verify that the issue is real and still present.

03

Track

Follow verifications, discussion, updates and progress over time.

04

Fix

Record resolution evidence and highlight problems that are fixed or reported again.
