Using this site

Your profile and contributions are stored locally in your browser tab. Reports, comments and updates are not published to the community or sent to authorities. Local confirmations do not count as public verification, and local fix updates do not officially resolve an issue.

Public-source material

Selected issue information remains attributed to its public source. The information may reflect the date of the original record rather than current conditions. This is not an official government record or an emergency service.

No guarantee of action

Showing an issue does not promise that an authority has received it or will act. Outcome summaries describe the public records shown on the site.

Responsible use

Use factual descriptions and respect people’s privacy. Do not impersonate others, manufacture evidence or misuse the website.
