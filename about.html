About FixMyLGA

Local problems deserve clear evidence, community attention and visible progress.

FixMyLGA is a community platform for documenting and tracking local physical problems across Nigeria.

Each issue can be followed over time through community verification, discussion, updates and evidence of progress or resolution.

FixMyLGA focuses on the everyday problems that affect how our communities live and function, including damaged roads, flooding and drainage, waste and sanitation, and public infrastructure.

The platform is built around civic responsibility, community participation and public accountability. It gives residents a way to document what is happening around them, add evidence, follow progress and build a public record of the issues that matter to their communities.

Every report contributes to a clearer picture of where attention is needed, what action has been taken, what has been resolved and what continues to affect residents over time.

FixMyLGA is about citizens taking an active role in the places they call home. By making local issues visible and progress easier to follow, the goal is to strengthen trust, encourage responsibility, support better local government and help communities contribute to the progress of Nigeria.

Project and developer

FixMyLGA is a full-stack civic-tech project built by Emmanuel Oberabor using Django 5.2 and PostgreSQL/PostGIS, with evidence and media handling, moderation, community verification, authority-response tracking, scoped security controls and 288 passing automated tests.
