Privacy

Your contributions stay in your browser tab.

Updated 10 September 2026

Your profile and contributions

Your display name, chosen home LGA, report text, comments, confirmations, fix updates and followed LGAs are kept in temporary storage for this browser tab. They survive navigation and refresh within the tab. This is a local profile, not an authenticated account.

Use Reset profile to clear your information. Closing the tab normally clears its storage, although a browser may restore it when restoring a closed tab or session. If browser storage is unavailable, changes last only on the current page.

Files and location

Selected photos and videos are previewed in memory on the reporting page. File contents are never saved to your profile or uploaded. No form information is sent to authorities or a live reporting service, and this site does not request your GPS location.

Website hosting

The hosting provider receives ordinary page and asset requests and may retain technical access logs, including IP addresses, for security and operation. See GitHub Pages data collection. The site has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Public issue records

The selected public issue records and still images are separate from your local contributions. Original-source attribution is retained. Private reporter details, precise private coordinates and internal notes are not included.

External links and contact

Original sources, WhatsApp and my website have their own privacy practices. For privacy questions, contact Emmanuel Oberabor.
